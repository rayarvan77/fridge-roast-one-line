One line, 102 characters, roasting a smart fridge: its camera catches a 2 a.m. snack raid and texts the owner to snitch — to the milk. The joke only works because the fridge is smart: a plain fridge cannot photograph you, message your phone, or gossip about your dairy.

## Why this line

- **On subject, no doubt:** camera, text message, phone — the roast needs the fridge to be smart, or it falls apart.
- **Specific, not generic:** no "cool", "chill", or leftover jokes; the gag is the fridge's camera plus its app acting like a tattletale roommate.
- **The turn:** the fridge does not shame you itself — it threatens to tell the milk, the one witness that lives inside it.
- **Tight:** 102 of the allowed 200 characters, one sentence, no filler words.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
